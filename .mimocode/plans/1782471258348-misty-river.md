## What the project is

AURUM is a **quantitative trading system** with three tiers:
1. **Web server** (Node.js + Express + MySQL) — serves frontend, manages users, proxies AI signals
2. **Frontend** (vanilla HTML/CSS/JS at `public/`) — trading UI, course platform, admin panel
3. **MT5 Bridge** (separate Python app, user's local machine) — connects to MetaTrader 5 terminal

The system enables AI-driven trading: users configure AI providers (DeepSeek/GPT/etc.), the server collects MT5 market data via WebSocket, sends it to AI models for analysis, and the resulting signals can be automatically or manually executed through the MT5 terminal.
